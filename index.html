Featured Research

Pedestrian attribute recognition, from the air

Research — In Progress
Visibility-Guided Multimodal Knowledge Graph (VGMKG) for Pedestrian Attribute Recognition from Drone-Captured Imagery
KFUPM & SDAIA Joint Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (JRCAI) · Supervisor: Dr. Hussein Samma · June 2026 – Present

Problem

Recognizing human attributes (like clothing, accessories, and pose-related traits) from drone-captured pedestrian imagery, where body regions are frequently occluded or out of frame — a challenge standard attribute-recognition pipelines don't account for.

My Role

Research Intern leading this subtrack: designing the model architecture, implementing the training/evaluation pipeline on HPC, running ablation studies, and co-authoring the resulting paper.

Approach

A multimodal pipeline combining independent DINOv2 ViT-B/16 encoders (global and region-level crops), a graph neural network over a knowledge graph of region/part/attribute nodes, and Sentence-BERT text embeddings for attribute descriptions. A skeleton-based visibility module built on pre-computed COCO-style keypoint data reasons about which body regions are actually visible, replacing an earlier MediaPipe-based approach. Along the way, I diagnosed and corrected a data-leakage issue from random-splitting augmented images of the same individuals, adopting an identity-disjoint evaluation protocol instead.

Munsit — Real-Time Arabic Accessibility System
June 2026

An AI system bridging communication gaps for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals in Arabic-speaking environments. Three modules run in parallel: Arabic speech-to-text via Whisper, sign-language detection using MediaPipe hand landmarks and a fully connected neural network trained on 4,507 samples across 28 Arabic letter classes, and environmental audio classification via CNN. Unified into a real-time pipeline with a Streamlit interface and text-to-speech output.
